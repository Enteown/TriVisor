## __Wanna help?__
__Still, there are a huge quantity of embeddable websites that we lacked to support in our plug-in, you can add one of them, for the community.__

__But at first, try to understand what's happening in the `generateEmbedUrl()`, how actually it works. Must keep in mind, it just opens a specific content (like Post, Reel or Video/Playlist from direct links) but never the full-fledged website itself.__

__Currently available:__
- __CodePen__
- __Dailymotion__
- __Facebook (sharing link isn't supported)__
- __Instagram__
- __JSFiddle__
- __Twitter__
- __Vimeo__
- __YouTube__

__Also our Proxy (better call it a site scrapper) is very basic, which need more upgrades.__

> [!WARNING]
> __Obtaining special permissions (like camera or microphone) here is simply impossible.__
